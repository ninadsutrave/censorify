# Censorify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Censorify is a browser extension that hides swear words on the web pages you visit. This policy covers the Chrome, Edge and Firefox versions.

## What Censorify reads

To censor a page, Censorify reads the visible text of the page in your browser and replaces swear words in place. This happens entirely on your device. Page text is never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

## What Censorify stores

Censorify saves its settings in your browser's local extension storage:

- whether censoring is on or off
- the censor style (asterisks, funny words or a custom placeholder) and the placeholder you chose
- the password you set to lock the settings
- your "always censor" and "never censor" word lists

These settings stay on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the extension.

## What Censorify does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not track your browsing history or activity.
- It does not use analytics, ads or any remote servers.
- It does not load code from outside the extension.
